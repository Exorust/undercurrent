    Areas: the broadest fields. Topics: research topics, the default for growth and links. Sub-topics: narrow clusters inside a topic.

    Colour by




    Growth: orange grows faster than CS, blue shrinks.

    Topic: one colour per area. Date: darker points are newer.

    Distance to A: lighter points are nearer to A (set A first).

    Lightness only: growth as light to dark grey, for colour-blind reading or print.

    layers ▾

    Map layers



    Topic nodes: one circle per topic, sized by papers in the window.

    Bridge links: lines between topics that share bridge papers; width = bridge papers.

    Phrase links: dashed lines for rising phrases shared by two topics, over all 36 months.

    Edges shown: how many of the strongest topic pairs get a bridge line.
