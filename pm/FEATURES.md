# Features — undercurrent (formerly field-radar; renamed 2026-11-07)
As of: 2026-11-06. Approved by user: yes (2026-11-06, "Approve as written"; libraries chosen: Tailwind + Inter, anime.js).

Sources of intent: `docs/designs/topic-workbench.md` (design, with the 13 amendments), `docs/designs/topic-workbench-architecture.md` (arch), `docs/designs/topic-workbench-ui.md` (ui), `docs/designs/wireframe/sketch.html`. The user's one-sentence goal: "I want to see overarching topics that are coming in and people are publishing, and then I also want to see how different topics connect."

State is as of today: nothing from v2 is built. "partial" means a v1 piece exists that v2 changes or absorbs.

## User types
- **Researcher** (the user, reading): opens the page, sees which topics are arriving and which are connecting, lassos regions, compares two selections, asks for merge ideas, saves selections for next month.
- **Operator** (the same user, maintaining): runs `./run_all.sh` monthly, starts and stops `radar.serve`, resolves locks, reruns the pipeline without losing saved selections and ideas.

### Paper signals and filters (friend's requests, 2026-11-07; approved 2026-11-07: "The rest seem great. I want you to execute on that.")

User's answers 2026-11-07: D1 yes (author comment field); D2 done (key in `.env`, gitignored, mode 600); D3 yes; D4 yes, 30 papers minimum "maybe even more if it's too noisy" — the PM raises it (50, then 100) if the list at 30 has more than 3,000 entries or obvious junk (department-level duplicates, unnamed ids). D3 gate result (spike S3, 500 papers): 14.0 % of all references land in the corpus (28.7 % of references with an arXiv id), below the 30 % written above; mean 5.96 in-corpus references per paper, about 2.74 M edges over the corpus; by year 3.8 % / 16.8 % / 21.4 % for 2024 / 2025 / 2026 papers. PM decision: build F49(d) anyway. The 30 % gate was the wrong test: the share is low because the corpus starts in Nov 2023 and most references are older, which does not weaken topic-pair counts; 2.74 M edges is enough for every level-1 pair. Known bias, stated on the page: citation links undercount topics whose papers are mostly from 2024. Fetch rule from S3: batches of 100 (500 silently truncates nested references at about 10,000 per response). Perf numbers come from `window.__radar` (existing: `firstDrawMs`, `maxFrameMs`; new: `filterApplyMs`).

What exists today: `s2_meta.citations` and `influential` (Semantic Scholar, fetched once per paper by `radar/enrich.py`; never refreshed), shown on paper lines and used to sort bridge papers; exported as `citations.bin` (Uint16 per row). Not stored anywhere: the arXiv comment field, author affiliations, the reference graph.
