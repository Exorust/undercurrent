# F18 — Colour modes and legend

Walk with: headless Chrome; read colours from the legend DOM and from `window.__radar.colourOf(row)`.

## Flow F18.1 — growth scale (default)
Preconditions: page loaded, default view.
| step | action | expected result (observable) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | read the legend | 7 colour swatches with texts `shrinking fast (under ×0.5)`, `shrinking`, `slightly shrinking`, `flat (×0.9 to ×1.1)`, `slightly growing`, `growing`, `growing fast (×2 or more)`, plus `new` and `too few papers to rate`; each swatch shows a count of topics in that step; counts sum to 116 |
| 2 | swatch hex values | `#2f62b8 #5a86c8 #8ea3c0 #8c8c8c #c8a57a #e3883a #ff6a1a`; no data `#333333` |
| 3 | pick a topic with `growth` 1.5 from `/topics`; `window.__radar.colourOf(row of one of its papers)` | `#e3883a` (step 6) |
| 4 | a topic with `is_new` | its points are `#ff6a1a` and its label carries the text badge `new` |
| 5 | a topic with fewer than 30 papers in both windows | `#333333` |

## Flow F18.2 — switch modes
Preconditions: page loaded.
| step | action | expected result (observable) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | choose colour `topic` | 20 distinct hues (one per area); the legend is replaced by the text `one colour per area; hover a point for its area` |
| 2 | choose `date` | greys only: all point colours have r = g = b; a 2023-11 paper is `#2b2b2b`, a last-complete-month paper `#f0f0f0` |
| 3 | choose `distance to A` with A empty | the option is disabled; its tooltip reads `Set A first` |
| 4 | set A (F24), choose `distance to A` | greys only; A's own points keep the A outline; the nearest 5% of sub-topics are `#f0f0f0` |
| 5 | tick `Lightness only` under the legend | growth colours become a 7-step grey ramp `#2b2b2b … #f2f2f2`; the legend shows the step number next to each swatch; the `new` badge remains text |
| 6 | reload | the chosen mode persists via `localStorage` (or resets to growth; either, but the behaviour is stated in the (i) popover) |

## Flow F18.3 — recolour time after a window change
Preconditions: default view.
| step | action | expected result (observable) |
|---|---|---|
