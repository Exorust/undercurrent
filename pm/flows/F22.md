# F22 — Links overlay and Links tab

Walk with: headless Chrome in the "How topics connect" preset; compare with `GET /links?level=1`.

## Flow F22.1 — nodes and edges
Preconditions: preset 2 active (F20.2).
| step | action | expected result (observable) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `window.__radar.layers()` | contains a node layer with 116 circles; radius of the largest = 13 px, smallest ≥ 4 px (`3 + 10·sqrt(n/n_max)`); fill = the topic's growth step; 1 px black stroke |
| 2 | edge count | equals `min(40, number of pairs with n_bridge ≥ 10)`; the number is printed next to the slider as `40 of 212 pairs` |
| 3 | one edge's width | `1 + 7·min(strength/s95, 1)` px where `s95` is the 95th percentile of strength among drawn edges; widest edge 8 px |
| 4 | edge colour | the pair's link-growth step; a `new` pair is `#ff6a1a` with a `new` text tag at the midpoint |
| 5 | move the slider to 10, then 200 | edges redraw to that count (or the max available); the count text updates |
| 6 | hover an edge | tooltip `<A> ↔ <B>: 412 bridge papers in window, share 1.4% (was 1.0%), link growth ×1.46` with real numbers equal to `/links` |
| 7 | toggle `Phrase links (all 36 months)` on | dashed edges `#d0d0d0`, 1.5 px, 4 on / 3 off, drawn for `phrase_links` pairs; where a pair also has a bridge edge the dashed line is offset about 4 px; hover shows `shared rising phrases: world action models (41 / 63 papers)` |
| 8 | no edge for pairs under 10 bridges | pick a `low` pair from `/links`; no line between its centroids |

## Flow F22.2 — Links tab
Preconditions: preset 2 active.
| step | action | expected result (observable) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | tab header line | `Bridge paper: 4 or more of its 14 nearest neighbours in the other topic. Width = strength, bridges / √(papers₁ × papers₂). Growth = change in bridge share vs the prior window.` with the real T from `/meta` in place of 4 |
| 2 | row order | all `new` rows first, then by link growth descending, then a collapsed group `Pairs with fewer than 10 bridge papers (growth not computed) (N)` |
| 3 | top 5 rows | carry the badge numbers shown at edge midpoints |
| 4 | columns | `#`, `topic 1`, `topic 2`, `bridges`, `share now`, `share before`, `link growth`, `status`; `new` rows show the `new` tag in the growth cell with tooltip `New link: 10 or more bridge papers in this window, fewer than 3 in the prior window.` |
| 5 | expand the collapsed group | rows show `bridges` and `strength`, no growth; clicking one opens its evidence (F23) with the "too few" line |
| 6 | phrase links section | below the bridge list, header `Phrase links use all 36 months and do not follow the window. A phrase links two topics when 10+ papers and 20%+ of its papers are in each, and it spans at most 4 topics.`; one row per pair with its phrases |
| 7 | click a row | F23.1 starts; the matching edge is highlighted |
