## Flow F43.2 — verbs and selections
| step | action | expected result (observable) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | click `Search` in the sidebar, or press `/` | focus moves to the composer input (one search box only) |
| 2 | type `LLM Agent Tools` | suggestions open above the composer in groups: `Topics` (matches topic and sub-topic names; ≥ 1 hit here), `Rising phrases`, `Search titles for "LLM Agent Tools"` |
| 3 | pick the topic suggestion | the topic is selected (same as a map click); inspector filled; it appears in `Selections` |
| 4 | click `Lasso` (sidebar or composer chip) or press `L` | lasso mode on: the row and chip show active; a 28 px hint bar at the top of the map reads `Drag around an area to select it · Esc to cancel` |
| 5 | click `Compare A·B` with nothing selected | guided compare starts: hint bar `Pick the first thing to compare: click a topic, search, or lasso`; the next selection becomes A, then the hint reads `Now pick the second`; the next selection becomes B; then the A vs B block shows at the top of the inspector, scrolled into view |
| 6 | click `Compare A·B` with only `current` set | `current` becomes A and the hint asks for the second |
| 7 | in `Selections`, hover a row | buttons `A`, `B`, `×` on the row: make it A, make it B, remove |
| 8 | click `Overview` | all selections cleared, preset `What is arriving` applied and shown as pressed, inspector shows the window summary |
| 9 | click `Connections` or press `2` | the `How topics connect` preset; the row shows active; clicking again or `1` returns |
| 10 | first selection ever | a one-line note in the hint bar for 4 s: `Selected. It is also A, ready to compare.` |
