# F49 — Citations

Walk with: `duckdb` on a copy of the database; headless Chrome on static and local modes.

## Flow F49.1 — data
| step | action | expected result (observable) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `select max(fetched_at), min(fetched_at) from s2_meta` after the monthly refresh | no paper older than 35 days since its last fetch |
| 2 | `select month, quantile_cont(cit_pct, .5) from paper_cites group by 1` | median about 50 in every month (percentile within the month); null citations get null percentile, not 0 |
| 3 | spot check: a paper with 0 citations in a month where most papers have 0 | its percentile equals the tie rule written in `radar/citations.py` (average rank) |
| 4 | `select count(*) from cite_refs` (only if D3 = yes) | both ends of every edge are in `papers`; no self-citations; count printed by the fetcher matches |
| 5 | `select * from topic_citelinks where level=1 order by n_cites desc limit 3` | pairs exist and `n_cites` = count of `cite_refs` edges whose ends sit in the two topics (check one pair by hand) |

## Flow F49.2 — page
| step | action | expected result (observable) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | read any paper line | `12 citations · top 8 % of its month` |
| 2 | topic inspector | `Citations: median at the 54th percentile of their month · 14 % in the top tenth` |
| 3 | Topics sheet | a sortable `Cited` column (median percentile) |
| 4 | Filter → minimum citation control `Top 10 % of their month` (options: any, top 50 %, top 25 %, top 10 %) | only those papers stay bright; URL gains `cit=10` |
| 5 | Links sheet (if D3 = yes) | a `Citations` column next to `Bridges`; sorting by it works |
| 6 | open a link pair (if D3 = yes) | evidence line `42 bridge papers · 310 citations between these topics`, plus `Most-cited across the pair:` 5 papers, each with the topic it cites into |
| 7 | the page text explains percentile once | the (i) note: `Citation counts from Semantic Scholar, refreshed monthly. Percentile compares a paper with others submitted the same month.` |
