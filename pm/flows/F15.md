# F15 — Compare A and B: `GET /distance`, `GET /colour/dist`

Walk with: `curl`.

## Flow F15.1 — distance between two topic selections
Preconditions: `sel_a` = topic 63 (level 1), `sel_b` = a topic the Links tab shows as strongly linked to 63, `sel_c` = an unrelated topic.
| step | action | expected result (observable) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `GET /distance?a=<a>&b=<b>` | `{cos_dist, n_a, n_b, overlap: 0, n_bridges, bridges: [...]}`; `0 < cos_dist < 1`; `n_bridges` equals `GET /link` for the pair ± papers outside both (documented) |
| 2 | `GET /distance?a=<a>&b=<c>` | `cos_dist` larger than step 1; `n_bridges` smaller |
| 3 | `GET /distance?a=<a>&b=<a>` | `cos_dist` 0 ± 1e-6; `overlap` = `n_a` |
| 4 | `bridges` entries | ≤ 20, each a `paper` with `k_a >= T` and `k_b >= T` |
| 5 | recompute `cos_dist` from the mean 768-d vectors of both member sets | equal ± 1e-4 |
| 6 | `GET /distance?a=<a>` (missing b) | `400`; unknown id → `404` |

## Flow F15.2 — distance between two lassos that do not touch
Preconditions: two lasso selections on either side of a visible gap.
| step | action | expected result (observable) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `GET /distance?a=&b=` | `overlap: 0`; `n_bridges` > 0 is allowed even though no paper is in both (bridges may lie in the gap); each bridge's `row` is in neither selection or in one |
