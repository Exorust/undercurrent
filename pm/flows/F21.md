# F21 — Topics tab

Walk with: headless Chrome; compare table cells with `GET /topics`.

## Flow F21.1 — table content and sort
Preconditions: default view.
| step | action | expected result (observable) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | read the tab header line | `Ranked: topics with 200+ papers in the window.` |
| 2 | columns | `#` (badge number or blank), `topic`, `area`, `papers (window)`, `prior`, `growth vs CS`, `arriving sub-topics` |
| 3 | row count | 116 (scrollable within the 248 px body) |
| 4 | first 5 rows | carry badge numbers 1–5 matching the map badges; rows with `recent < 200` have no badge even if their growth is higher, and sit in growth order among the rest |
| 5 | one row's numbers | equal the `/topics` entry (`recent`, `prior`, `growth` to 2 decimals); growth cell uses the step colour; a `new` topic shows the `new` tag instead of a number |
| 6 | click the `papers (window)` header | sorted by that column descending; click again → ascending; the sort arrow shows |
| 7 | arriving chips under a topic name | chip text `world action models · new` or `agent harnesses ×3.1`; tooltip on a `new` chip reads `New sub-topic: started inside this window (onset Jun 2026), 30 or more papers now, fewer than 5 before.` with the real onset month |

## Flow F21.2 — row and chip clicks
Preconditions: F21.1 passed, nothing selected.
| step | action | expected result (observable) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | click a topic row | the topic becomes selection A (F24 rule); the map draws its hull; the row is highlighted |
| 2 | click a chip | the map zooms to that sub-topic (300 ms animation; instant with `prefers-reduced-motion`), outlines it, and the inspector shows the sub-topic (`Sub-topic, in topic <parent> (share 71%)`) |
| 3 | keyboard: focus the table, press ↓ three times and Enter | the fourth row is selected (same as clicking it) |

## Flow F21.3 — other levels
Preconditions: level set to areas (20) or sub-topics (368).
| step | action | expected result (observable) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | level = sub-topics | 368 rows; the `arriving sub-topics` column is empty; the ranking floor header changes to the level's floor or hides |
| 2 | level = areas | 20 rows; arriving chips list sub-topics by their level-2 parent |
