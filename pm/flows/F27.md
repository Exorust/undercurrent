# F27 — Inspector

Walk with: headless Chrome; compare text with `/select` and `/topics` responses.

## Flow F27.1 — selection view
Preconditions: a topic selected as A.
| step | action | expected result (observable) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | header | the label and `Topic, in area <area>`; for a sub-topic `Sub-topic, in topic <parent> (share 71%)`; for a lasso the lasso label; for a phrase the phrase label |
| 2 | numbers | `papers in window: N (prior M)`; `×2.1 after correction (raw ×2.9; all CS grew ×1.36)`; the growth number uses the step colour; the footnote names the window (`May to Oct 2026 vs Nov 2025 to Apr 2026`) |
| 3 | monthly bars | 36 grey bars; the window months have a shaded background; hovering a bar shows `Jun 2026: 143 papers` |
| 4 | arriving sub-topics | chips as in the Topics tab; clicking one zooms (F21.2) |
| 5 | distinctive phrases | up to 20 chips with `k` papers each; clicking a chip runs that phrase search (F26.1); tooltip shows `share in selection 12% vs 0.4% in all papers (z 7.2)` |
| 6 | top cited / newest | 10 each; title links to arxiv.org; citations and month shown |
| 7 | links to other topics | up to 10 rows `<topic> · 31% of neighbours`, from `topics[].nbr_share`; clicking opens that topic as the current selection |
| 8 | buttons | `Save`, `Set as A` / `Set as B` (as applicable), `Ideas between A and B` (disabled until both set) |
