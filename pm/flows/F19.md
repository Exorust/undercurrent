# F19 — Labels by zoom level

Walk with: headless Chrome; count visible labels via `window.__radar.visibleLabels()` (returns the texts the TextLayer drew after collision filtering).

## Flow F19.1 — label sets per zoom
Preconditions: default view, level = topics.
| step | action | expected result (observable) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | at full-map fit (zoom < 2×) | `visibleLabels()` has ≤ 25 entries: the 20 area names (14 px, weight 600) plus the 5 badge topics (12 px) |
| 2 | zoom to 3× on a dense region | labels are topic names, 12 px, between 10 and 50 visible, none overlapping (bounding boxes do not intersect) |
| 3 | zoom to 6× | labels are sub-topic names, 11 px |
| 4 | screenshot at each step | labels have a 3 px `#111` halo; text is readable over points |

## Flow F19.2 — priority
Preconditions: a topic selected as A (F24).
| step | action | expected result (observable) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | zoom to 3× where A would collide with a bigger neighbour | A's label is drawn; the neighbour's may be dropped |
| 2 | badge topics at full fit | all 5 badge labels drawn even where they collide with area names (area name yields) |
